It was never willpower. The game was quietly rigged against you.

You tried keto, but the cravings came back louder. You counted every calorie, but the mental chatter never stopped. Here is why.

When you lose weight, your body fights to put it back. Hunger hormones like ghrelin climb. The signals that tell your brain you are full get quieter. Your metabolism slows and stays slow.

You were not failing the diet. Your biology was defending the weight, on a schedule, every single time. No amount of discipline out-argues a hormone.

What can actually turn the volume down.
